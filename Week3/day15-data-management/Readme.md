# 🚀 Salesforce Summer Program – Day 15 Data Management

---

# 📖 Data Quality Problems

## 1. Duplicate Student Records
May cause duplicate notifications and incorrect reports.

## 2. Missing Email Address
Students may not receive important updates.

## 3. Wrong Department Assignment
Students may be assigned to incorrect courses.

## 4. Invalid Attendance Values
Attendance reports become inaccurate.

## 5. Duplicate Course Allocation
Students may be enrolled multiple times.

## 6. Missing Student Name
Records become difficult to identify.

## 7. Incorrect Fee Information
May cause payment and billing issues.

## 8. Invalid Phone Number
Communication with students becomes difficult.

## 9. Incorrect Course Details
Leads to confusion in course management.

## 10. Outdated Student Information
Reports and decisions become unreliable.

---

# 🔄 Data Migration Discussion

When moving from Excel sheets to Salesforce:

### Challenges
- Duplicate records
- Missing data
- Different data formats
- Invalid records
- Incorrect field mapping

### Solutions
- Clean data before migration
- Validate records
- Remove duplicates
- Check field mappings carefully

---

# 🛡️ Duplicate Prevention Ideas

- Use Validation Rules
- Check for existing records before import
- Use unique student IDs
- Review imported data regularly
- Enable duplicate management features

---

# ⚠️ Enterprise Risks of Bad Data

If 50,000 student records are imported incorrectly:

- Wrong notifications may be sent
- Attendance reports become inaccurate
- Fee calculations may be incorrect
- Reporting and analytics become unreliable
- Business decisions may be affected

---

# 💭 Reflection

Clean and reliable data is critical because enterprise systems depend on accurate information for reporting, automation, communication, and decision-making. Poor data quality can lead to errors, inefficiency, and loss of trust.
