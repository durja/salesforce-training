# 🚀 Salesforce Summer Program – Day 17 Agentforce & AI

## 🤖 Agentforce Summary
Agentforce is Salesforce's AI platform that helps businesses build AI agents to automate tasks, answer questions, and perform actions using enterprise data.

---

## 💡 AI Agent Use Cases
- AI Attendance Assistant
- AI Course Advisor
- AI Placement Recommendation System
- AI Student Support Assistant
- AI Faculty Schedule Assistant

---

## 🔄 AI Workflow
User Question → AI Agent → Flow/Apex → Database → Response → Action

AI agents use Salesforce data, Flows, and Apex to automate business processes.

---

## ⚠️ Risks of Enterprise AI
- Hallucinations (wrong answers)
- Privacy risks
- Incorrect automation
- Bias in decisions
- Over-automation

Enterprises should monitor and validate AI actions carefully.

---

## 💭 Reflection
AI agents can improve productivity and automate repetitive tasks, but human oversight is important for accuracy, security, and reliability.
