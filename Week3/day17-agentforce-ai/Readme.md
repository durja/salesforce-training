# 🤖 Agentforce Summary

Agentforce is Salesforce's AI platform that enables organizations to build AI agents capable of understanding requests, accessing business data, performing actions, and assisting users automatically.

It combines AI, Flows, Apex, and enterprise data to automate business processes.

# 🔄 AI Workflow Explanation

User asks a question →  
AI Agent receives request →  
Flow or Apex processes business logic →  
Database retrieves required data →  
AI generates response →  
Action is executed automatically

### How AI Integrates with Enterprise Systems
AI agents use business data, Flows, Apex, and automation tools to provide intelligent responses and perform tasks automatically.

---

# ⚠️ Risks of Enterprise AI

## Hallucinations
AI may provide incorrect information.

## Wrong Automation
Incorrect actions may be executed automatically.

## Privacy Risks
Sensitive data may be exposed.

## Bias
AI decisions may be unfair or inaccurate.

## Incorrect Approvals
AI may approve requests incorrectly.

## Over-Automation
Too much automation may reduce human oversight.

### Why Enterprises Need Control
AI systems require validation, monitoring, security controls, and human review to ensure reliable and safe operation.

---

# 💭 Reflection

AI agents can significantly improve enterprise software by automating repetitive tasks, assisting users, improving productivity, and supporting decision-making. However, human oversight and governance will remain important to ensure accuracy, security, and trust.
